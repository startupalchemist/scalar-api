# 3D SECTION – PINNED SCROLL BEHAVIOR

## SCROLL LOGIC

When the user reaches the 3D section:

1. Vertical page scroll is temporarily locked.
2. Section becomes "pinned" to viewport.
3. User scroll input now controls animation progress.
4. Once animation completes (Impact Level = 0%), normal vertical scroll resumes.

This must feel:
- Smooth
- Controlled
- Cinematic
- Not abrupt

Use easing curves.
No snapping.

---

# ANIMATION STRUCTURE

## Initial State (Scroll Progress 0%)

Car visible.
Damage fully present.

Left-side overlay:

IMPACT LEVEL  
100%

Typography:
White or muted gray.
No red.

---

## During Scroll (Progress 0% → 100%)

As user scrolls:

- Car rotates subtly (max 20° Y-axis)
- Hail damage fades via shader blend
- Windshield crack fades last
- Impact Level counts down smoothly:
  100 → 0

Counter Behavior:
- Smooth interpolation
- No ticking effect
- No bouncing numbers
- No sound effects

---

## Final State (Progress 100%)

IMPACT LEVEL  
0%

Text fades in:

Restored.

Pause 800ms.

Then:

Subtle red contour sweep traces car body lines once.
Duration: ~1.2 seconds.
No looping.
No flashing.
No pulsing.

Red is accent only.

---

# POST-ANIMATION CTA

After contour sweep completes:

Primary Button:
Get My Assessment

Microcopy:
Takes about a minute.

This CTA appears within the pinned section before scroll resumes.

---

# SCROLL RELEASE

After final state holds for ~1.5 seconds:

Pinned state releases.
Vertical page scroll resumes naturally.

No jump.
No snap.

# RED ACCENT RULES

Red is used ONLY for:

- “48 HOURS.”
- Thin button hover underline
- One-time contour sweep at 0% impact
- Minimal nav highlight

Red must not:
- Pulse
- Glow continuously
- Animate repeatedly
- Appear in counter numbers

Luxury = restraint.

---

# BACKGROUND RULES

No carbon fiber texture.
No visible weave patterns.

Allowed:
- Subtle grain overlay (very low opacity)
- Soft vignette
- Matte charcoal environment
- Glossy floor reflection under vehicle

---

# EXPERIENCE GOAL

User flow:

1. Calm authority (Hero)
2. Visual emotional engagement (Pinned 3D)
3. Logical curiosity (Impact counter)
4. Confidence (Restored)
5. Action (AI Assessment CTA)
6. Guarantee reinforcement (Later section)

The experience must feel:
- Engineered
- Controlled
- Premium
- Confident
- Not gimmicky

No puns.
No countdown clocks.
No novelty animations.
