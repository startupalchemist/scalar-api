## 3D SECTION – ERASE THE STORM

Full-screen pinned scroll section.

---

### 3D REQUIREMENTS

Models:
• car_damaged.glb
• car_clean.glb

Must:
• Be identical geometry alignment
• Same pivot point
• Same scale
• No brand logos
• Generic performance coupe silhouette

Environment:
• Hex LED wall background
• Glossy black reflective floor
• Matte charcoal surroundings

---

### SCROLL BEHAVIOR

Scroll controls:

• Rotation max 20° Y axis
• Shader blend damaged → clean
• Windshield crack fades last
• Red contour highlight appears at completion

Scroll 0:
Fully damaged

Scroll 1:
Fully restored

---

### SHADER LOGIC

Uniform:
float uProgress

Fragment:
gl_FragColor = mix(damagedColor, cleanColor, uProgress);

Windshield fade:
if (progress > 0.85)
  windshieldOpacity = 1.0 - ((progress - 0.85) / 0.15);

---

### LIGHTING

• Soft key light
• Cool rim light
• Subtle red accent light
• HDRI studio map
• Floor reflectivity 0.15

No bloom.
No dramatic flare.
No excessive glow.
