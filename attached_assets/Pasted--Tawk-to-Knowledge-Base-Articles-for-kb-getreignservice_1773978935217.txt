# Custom Turf Installation

## What is custom turf?
Custom turf is high-quality artificial grass that looks and feels natural while requiring minimal maintenance.

## Benefits
- No mowing, watering, or fertilizing needed
- Stays green year-round
- Pet and child-friendly options available
- Drought-resistant solution for Texas climate
- Reduces water bills and maintenance costs
- Environmentally friendly alternative to traditional lawns

## Our Process
1. **Consultation:** We assess your space and discuss your specific needs
2. **Design:** Custom layout planning to maximize your outdoor area
3. **Ground Preparation:** Proper drainage and base preparation
4. **Professional Installation:** Expert placement and securing
5. **Finishing:** Detailed edge work and final brushing

## Maintenance
Our artificial turf requires simple occasional rinsing and brushing to keep it looking fresh. No mowing, watering, fertilizing, or weed control needed!

## Lifespan
With proper care, our custom turf installations typically last 15-20 years, providing exceptional long-term value.

## FAQ
**Q: Is artificial turf safe for children and pets?**
A: Yes! We offer pet-friendly and child-safe turf options that are non-toxic and designed for comfort and durability.

**Q: Can artificial turf be installed on any surface?**
A: Most yards can accommodate turf installation. Our team will assess your specific situation during the consultation.

**Q: How does turf handle heavy rain?**
A: Our turf installations include professional drainage systems to handle Texas rain events efficiently.

# Foundation Repair

## Signs of Foundation Issues
- Cracks in walls (especially diagonal cracks)
- Cracks in floors or concrete slabs
- Doors that stick or don't latch properly
- Uneven or sloping floors
- Gaps around window frames or exterior doors
- Separation between walls and ceiling
- Rotating or leaning chimney

## Our Approach
1. **Thorough inspection:** Comprehensive evaluation of your foundation issues
2. **Customized repair plan:** Solutions tailored to your specific situation
3. **Professional implementation:** Experienced technicians using proven methods
4. **Follow-up evaluation:** Ensuring the solution has addressed the problem

## Methods Used
We utilize multiple proven foundation repair techniques:
- Pier and beam reinforcement
- Slab foundation repair
- Drainage correction
- Soil stabilization
- Mudjacking and slabjacking
- Steel pier installation

## Warranty
We offer comprehensive warranties on foundation work, giving you peace of mind that your investment is protected.

## Prevention Tips
- Maintain consistent soil moisture around your foundation
- Ensure proper drainage away from your home
- Address plumbing leaks promptly
- Consider foundation watering systems for clay soils
- Schedule regular foundation inspections

## FAQ
**Q: How can I tell if my foundation problems are serious?**
A: Any foundation movement warrants professional evaluation. We offer free inspections to assess the severity.

**Q: How long does foundation repair take?**
A: Most residential foundation repairs can be completed in 2-5 days, depending on the extent of the issues.

**Q: Will foundation issues come back after repair?**
A: With proper repair methods and attention to underlying causes, foundation repairs should provide a permanent solution.

## General Questions

### How do I request a quote?

Yes, Reign Services is fully licensed and insured, including general liability insurance and workers' compensation. We're happy to provide verification upon request.

Yes, we partner with reputable financing companies to offer various payment plans. Ask about current options during your consultation.

### How do you handle project delays or unexpected issues?
We build buffer time into our schedules for unexpected challenges. If delays occur, we communicate promptly and transparently, providing updated timelines and solutions.

### Do you offer warranties?
Yes! All of our work comes with a workmanship warranty. Additionally, all materials and products we install maintain their original manufacturer warranties.

## Project-Specific Questions

### What is the typical ROI for a kitchen or bathroom remodel?
Kitchen remodels typically return 70-80% of their cost in home value, while bathroom remodels average 60-70% ROI. However, both significantly improve daily living quality and home marketability.

### How long does a typical renovation project take?
Project timelines vary widely based on scope:
- Small projects (e.g., fence installation): 1-3 days
- Medium projects (e.g., bathroom remodel): 2-4 weeks
- Large projects (e.g., kitchen remodel): 3-6 weeks
- Comprehensive renovations: 2-3 months

### Do I need to move out during renovation?
Most clients can remain in their homes during renovation. We isolate work areas and minimize disruption. For extensive whole-home renovations, temporary relocation might be more comfortable.

### How do you ensure quality control?
We implement a multi-point inspection process, with quality checks at each project phase. Our project managers oversee all work, and we don't consider a project complete until it meets our high standards and your approval.

### Do you handle all necessary permits?
Yes, we manage the entire permit process for our clients, including submission, revisions if needed, and final inspections.
